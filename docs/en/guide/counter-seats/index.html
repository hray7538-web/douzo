Counter seats and why they are hard to book

Eight seats, one chef, one phone. That is the whole system — and it is why these are the best meals in the city.

  • Write in your own language
  • Confirmed within a day
  • No app, no login

What do you want to book?

We'll reply with the confirmation. If the first place is full, we try the next.

Why you need this

  • There is no system to put online. The chef is cooking. The phone rings between orders. Adding a booking site would mean hiring someone.
  • Ask early. Two to four weeks ahead for the good ones. Same-day is possible but it is luck.
  • Be exact about the number. One extra person is 12% of the room. Changing it later is not a small change.
  • Arrive on time, not early. There is usually nowhere to wait.
